1. About Buy Rent Malazi

Buy Rent Malazi is an online listing platform. We help property owners, landlords, hosts and independent agents advertise properties to buy, rent or stay in (Malazi), and we help people find them.

We are not an estate agent, broker, landlord, host or seller. We do not own, inspect, value, verify or manage any listed property. We are not a party to any sale, lease, rental or booking agreement made between users, and we do not collect or hold purchase money, rent or deposits.

2. Who can list

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must own the property or have the owner's written authority to advertise it. We may ask for proof at any time.
  • Agents must hold any registration or licence required by Tanzanian law, and must state that they are acting as an agent.

3. Your listing

  • All details (price in TSh, location, size, features and availability) must be accurate, current and not misleading.
  • Photos must show the actual property, and you must have the right to use them.
  • You must update or remove the listing as soon as the property is sold, let or no longer available.

4. What is not allowed

  • Fake, duplicate or bait listings, or properties you have no right to advertise.
  • Asking anyone to pay a deposit, viewing fee or "holding fee" before they have viewed the property and verified ownership.
  • Discriminatory, offensive or unlawful content, or listings for any illegal use.
  • Misusing other users' contact details or sending spam.

5. Review and removal

We may review listings before or after they are published. We may edit formatting, ask for more information, or suspend or remove any listing or account that breaks these terms or that we reasonably believe is fraudulent. We may report suspected fraud to the relevant authorities.

6. Fees

Standard listings are free. If we introduce paid features (for example, featured placement), the price will be shown clearly before you choose them, and you will never be charged without agreeing first.

7. Dealing with other users safely

Any agreement is between you and the other user. Before paying money or signing anything, you should view the property in person, check the seller's or landlord's identity, and confirm ownership (for example, through the title deed and an official land registry search). Use a lawyer for property purchases. Never send money to someone you have not met or verified.

8. Liability

The platform is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, Buy Rent Malazi is not responsible for the accuracy of listings or for any loss arising from dealings between users. You agree to cover any claims, costs or losses that arise from content you post or from your breach of these terms.

9. Your content

You keep ownership of your photos and descriptions. By listing, you give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display, format and promote your listing on our website, apps and marketing channels while it is live.

10. Privacy Policy

What we collect: your name, phone number, email address, listing details and photos, and basic usage data (such as pages viewed).

Why we collect it: to publish your listing, pass genuine enquiries to you, prevent fraud and improve the service.

Who sees it: your listing is public. Your contact details are shared only with people who enquire, and with service providers who help us run the platform under confidentiality obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights: you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. We handle personal data in line with applicable Tanzanian law, including the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

11. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms. If changes are significant, we will notify listers before they take effect. These terms are governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania.
